Meet Maya

Movement with meaning.

Maya Bennett is a 500-hour registered yoga teacher with more than a decade of experience in Hatha, Vinyasa, and restorative practices. Her teaching is warm, grounded, and quietly encouraging.

After discovering yoga during a demanding chapter of her own life, Maya came to understand practice not as an escape, but as a way to meet life more fully.

Her classes weave intelligent sequencing, breath awareness, and spacious moments of stillness—giving you room to listen inward.
